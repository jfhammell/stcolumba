St Columba is hosting an Open House on October 5 to showcase the various activities and groups that use the facilities of the church. We will have the Girl Guides, the Irish Seniors Social Group of Ottawa, the Outouais Wood Carvers, Au Gré du Vent, ARC Studio School, the Manor Park Environmental Sustainability Committee and others.
