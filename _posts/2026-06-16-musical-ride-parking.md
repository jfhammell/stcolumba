St Columba will be offering our property for visitors parking to attend the RCMP Sunset Ceremonies Musical Ride, June 25-28.  Our location is excellent, just across the street.  

We ask those wishing to park to please consider a donation to the church.  We are able to accept cash or tap-to-pay. 

Come early as the space fills up fast.
